How Weapon Stats Work

Attack roll: d20 + the weapon's Attack stat, compared against the target's AC. Meeting or beating AC is a hit.

Damage roll: on a hit, roll the weapon's damage die and add its damage modifier.

Many weapons carry a perk — a special trick the weapon allows. Weaker hitters tend to carry the strongest perks; the heaviest hitters often need none at all. Saves named below use the difficulty scale from Rules & Terms (Easy 10 · Normal 13 · Hard 16 · Very Hard 20).

Each weapon also lists a Fits tag — a purely cosmetic suggestion for which class and race the weapon suits in look and spirit. It carries no mechanical bonus or restriction; any character can wield any weapon.
