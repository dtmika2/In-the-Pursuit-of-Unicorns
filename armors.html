How Armor Works

Armor Class (AC): the number an enemy's attack roll must meet or beat to hit you. Higher AC means you are harder to hit.

Heavier armor tends to grant a stronger benefit, but the heaviest sets carry a real drawback in exchange for the protection. Saves named below use the difficulty scale from Rules & Terms (Easy 10 · Normal 13 · Hard 16 · Very Hard 20).
