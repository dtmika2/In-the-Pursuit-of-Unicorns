# Factory Monsters — Stat Blocks

Five recurring enemy types found throughout the factory. Each spent so long working one division of the plant that they fused with the tools and gear of their trade — moving on instinct now, barely thinking. Use these as the random encounters the slot-machine roller (`factory-monsters-roll.html`) points to.

Combat follows the standard rules in `reference.md`: attacks roll **d20 + modifier vs AC**; saves use the listed DC against the target's relevant ability score.

---

## Janitor

**HP d12+8 · AC 13**

Sealed for so long inside an antique diving suit — riveted iron plating, a brass helmet with one fogged-over porthole, an air hose that used to lead somewhere and now just leads back into itself — that nobody remembers if there's still a person inside, or just the suit, walking. It was built to work the flooded lower levels, and it never really left them. Slow on land. Not slow underwater.


|                             |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attack — Wrench Crank**   | d20+3 vs AC · 1d8 damage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Utility — Sealed Suit**   | Passive. Immune to drowning, gas, and smoke — the helmet keeps its own air. Moves along the bottom of flooded rooms at full speed and can lie in wait there indefinitely. The weighted boots mean it cannot be knocked prone or pushed back.                                                                               |
| **Unique — Pressure Burst** | 1/encounter. Vents everything the suit has been holding in at once. Every creature within 10ft: Save Constitution 11 or take 1d6 damage and get knocked back 10ft in a scalding burst of air, rust, and stale water. Than start drawning - start death rolls - during them, you can act. You can be stabilized by an ally. |

## Smoker

**HP d12+7 · AC 10**

Its lungs gave out years ago and its body built a chimney instead. Doesn't fight in melee if it can help it — it just needs you to breathe.


|                                |                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attack — Tar Cough**         | Ranged, 20ft. d20+3 vs AC · 1d4 damage                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Utility — Standing Haze**    | Passive. Always stands inside a permanent cloud of its own smoke (5ft radius). The cloud lightly obscures — attacks against the Smoker while it's inside have disadvantage.                                    |
| **Unique — Black Lung Exhale** | 1/encounter. Exhales a 15ft cone of thick smoke. Each creature inside: Save Constitution 12 or take 1d6 damage and become **Choking** — unable to speak — the players cannot communicate about their strategy! |

## Foreman

**HP d12+15 · AC 13**

Built like the machinery it used to oversee — heavy, slow, fused at the shoulder with a hydraulic wrench it hasn't put down in years. Rank-and-file. No gun, no rank insignia, no clever tricks — just mass and a swing that doesn't stop for the first thing it hits.


|                           |                             |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Attack — Wrench Swing** | d20+4 vs AC · 1d10+2 damage |
|                           |                             |

## Foreman Lieutenant

**HP d12+10 · AC 12**

Promoted out of the line for reasons nobody explained. It carries something none of the other foremen do — a long iron tube it dug out of a sealed archive room, the kind of thing this world has no name for. It calls it the **Bang-Iron**. It likes how much faster people listen now.


|                              |                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attack — Bang-Iron**       | Ranged, 60ft. d20+4 vs AC · 1d10 damage                                                                                                                     |
| **Utility — Overtime**       | Passive. Any other factory monster within 10ft gets +1 to attack rolls. It doesn't inspire loyalty — it just makes everyone nearby too afraid to slow down. |
| **Unique — Mandatory Shift** | 1/encounter. Barks an order. One other factory monster within 30ft that has already acted this round immediately takes one more action, out of turn.        |

## Scientist

**HP d12+7 · AC 11**

Coat fused to the skin, one hand replaced outright by an injector rig still hooked to a bandolier of Elektrolit vials. It doesn't want to kill you. It wants a sample.


|                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attack — Injector Jab**    | d20+3 vs AC · 1d6 damage                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Utility — Field Patch**    | 1/encounter. Injects a damaged factory monster within 5ft with a stabilizing dose. Target regains 1d8 HP.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Unique — Bliss Injection** | On a hit with Injector Jab, instead of damage the Scientist can force the target to make a Hallucination Save (DC 13). On failure, the target loses 1 Sanity and must act on a DM-chosen hallucination for 1 round, per the standard Sanity rules. |

## Freak

**HP d12+11 · AC 9**

Whatever it used to operate, it's gone — replaced by too much limb, too much hunger, no clear shape. The factory didn't design this one. It just happened.


|                                |                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attack — Frenzied Claws**    | Two attacks per turn, roll separately. d20+3 vs AC · 1d8 damage each                                                                                 |
| **Utility — Feed**             | Passive. Whenever the Freak deals damage with Frenzied Claws, it regains half the damage dealt as HP.                                                |
| **Unique — Critical Mutation** | When reduced to 0 HP, the Freak doesn't simply die — He splits into two copies of itself. To prevent this, they have to burn or disenagrate somehow. |
